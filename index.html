Portal Explanation Guide

What Each Module Means

Simple explanation for dashboard, MPS, submission tracker, grades, learners, and reports.

1. Dashboard

The dashboard is the main summary page. It gives the school user a quick view of total learners, sections, pending outputs, learners needing support, learning progress, health watch, and submission status.

2. MPS Generator

The MPS Generator converts summative assessment scores into Mean Percentage Score. It answers: how did the class perform in WW, PT, EX, and overall weighted performance?

3. MPS Submission

MPS Submission means the generated MPS summary is recorded in the tracker. It serves as evidence that the class MPS was already prepared and submitted for monitoring.

4. Submission Tracker

The Submission Tracker is the monitoring log for required outputs. It shows pending and submitted items, including MPS reports, grade reports, SF reports, and other school requirements.

5. Grades

The Grades module computes learner results using the selected elementary DO 015 setting. It supports Kinder descriptive, Grade 1 descriptive, Grade 2 to 3 transition, and Grades 4 to 6 numerical grading.

6. Learner Support

Learner support flags show who may need remediation or follow-up. This helps teachers act early based on ST1, current MPS, or computed performance.

Dashboard Widget Dictionary

School Data Center Header: Shows the recognized school name, school ID, school year, district, and division. It confirms that the uploaded SF1 school identity has been read by the portal.
Learners KPI: Counts all learner profiles currently in the system. This should match the expected SF1 enrollment count after upload.
Male and Female KPI: Counts learners by the SF1 sex field. These cards help validate the male and female totals from SF1.
Sections KPI: Counts available class sections. Sections come from uploaded SF1 data or manual learner records.
Pending KPI: Counts tracker items still marked pending. This is the school output workload indicator.
Support KPI: Counts learners who need follow-up based on grade or MPS thresholds. This helps identify learners for remediation, guided practice, or monitoring.
Learning Progress Chart: Displays subject or learning area performance trends. It helps administrators and teachers see which learning areas need support.
MPS Summary Widget: Shows overall MPS plus WW, PT, and EX MPS. The Export button downloads the CSV. The Submit button posts the MPS summary to the tracker.
To Do Widget: Shows recent tracker requirements. This is a dashboard shortcut for pending or submitted school reports.
System Explanation Widget: Provides simple explanations of the main modules so users understand what the numbers and widgets represent.
Learner Status Chart: Groups learners by performance levels such as on track, near standard, or needing support. This gives a clearer picture than a single average.
Teacher Load Chart: Summarizes teaching load or scheduled minutes. It is useful for checking balance in teacher schedules.
Health Watch Chart: Summarizes SBFP or health-related records. It supports nutrition and learner welfare monitoring.
Quick Action Cards: Give shortcut access to Schedules, Grades, Health, and Inventory modules.

What Gets Exported or Posted

Dashboard export downloads the MPS CSV file. Dashboard submission posts the MPS summary into the Submission Tracker. The tracker record contains the overall MPS, WW MPS, PT MPS, EX MPS, learners below 75, submitter, and timestamp.
